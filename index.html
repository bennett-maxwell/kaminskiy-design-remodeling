Kaminskiy Design & Remodeling

The San Diego-based home-improvement brand founded by Sergei Kaminskiy in 2005. Its franchise arm, Kaminskiy Care and Repair (Kaminskiy Franchise Group, LLC), was formed in October 2024 in Frisco, Texas, and began offering franchises in its 2024 Franchise Disclosure Document (issued November 4, 2024). Research below is extracted from that FDD; figures below refer to the Kaminskiy Care and Repair franchise.

Risk Assessment

46 / 100

Moderate risk. Scored from five FDD-grounded factors (higher = riskier):

  • Legal cleanliness +2 — zero litigation (Item 3) and zero bankruptcy disclosures (Item 4).
  • Outlet stability +8 — brand-new franchise system: zero third-party franchisees at issuance (Exhibit C); 5 company-owned outlets at 2023 year-end with zero closures 2021–2023 (Item 20).
  • Fee burden +14 — 7% royalty + 1% brand fund + ~$7,500–$9,000/month in fixed fees and ad minimums; roughly 18–21% effective burden on average Item-19 revenue (Item 6).
  • Financial evidence +13 — Item 19 covers only n=2 company-owned outlets with a wide margin spread (22% vs 4%); figures unaudited; franchisor formed October 2024 with an unaudited opening balance sheet (Items 19, 21).
  • Trend momentum +9 — all growth has been company-owned; franchising only began in October 2024, so no franchisee validation exists yet.

Mitigating: zero litigation/bankruptcy disclosures; protected Designated Territory of 40,000–60,000 target households while in compliance (Item 12); trademarks claimed via 2024 USPTO application (Item 13); 10-year initial term with 2 × 5-year renewals (Item 17).

Watch: the assigned page brand is Kaminskiy Design & Remodeling, but the FDD franchise trade name is Kaminskiy Care and Repair — the FDD does not offer a “Design & Remodeling” franchise. Territory protection is non-exclusive: the franchisor reserves alternative channels and other-mark activity inside your territory with no compensation (Item 12). Brand Fund can rise to 3% (Item 6). Franchisees must comply with state contractor licensing (Item 1).
